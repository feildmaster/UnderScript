Hide the emotes you don't want to see.

* Every emote has its own setting in the Emotes section of the Chat settings. Turn one off to hide it in chat and in games.
* In games, the Emotes section of the Game settings can hide your opponent's emotes, only show emotes from friends, or hide emotes while you spectate.
