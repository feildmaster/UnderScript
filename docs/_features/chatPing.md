Insert custom words to get notified on.

`@underscript` is on the list by default. The first time a message pings you, a toast explains the custom notifications, and offers to open the settings, remove `@underscript`, or dismiss it.

![The custom notifications toast]({{ '/assets/features/chatPingNotice.png' | relative_url }})

Pings from chats you have closed can be ignored, so only the chats you have open will ping you.
