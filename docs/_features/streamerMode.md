title: Streamer mode
date: 2019-04-08
version: 0.25.0
credit: Ceveno
notice: This feature is hidden by default
---

Keeps private things off your stream.

To use it, set "Enable?" in the Streamer Mode section of the settings to "Yes", or "Yes (silent)" to skip the toast that says streamer mode is active. A Streamer Mode button then shows up in the UnderScript menu to turn it on and off.

While it is on:

* Your email is hidden on the Settings page
* Private messages are hidden. Each person who messages you is sent an automatic "do not disturb" reply, and a toast with an Open button lets you read their message. Moderators are always let through

"Private Messages" in the same section can allow them instead, or hide them without the toast.
