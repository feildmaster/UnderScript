A number next to the game's dust pile button shows how many cards are in the pile, one for you and one for your opponent.

Hovering a player's name in the battle log shows their dust pile count too.

The counter is hidden while spectating by default. Choose when to hide it with "Hide Dust Counter" on the Game page of the settings: never, while playing, while spectating, or always.
