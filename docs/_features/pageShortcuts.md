---
title: Page shortcuts
date: 2020-08-31
version: 0.40.0
---

Get around long lists faster, on every page that has pages.

* CTRL + Click the next or previous button to jump to the last or first page
* A drop down menu to pick the page you want

Both can be turned off in the settings. There is also a setting to stop the mouse wheel from changing pages in your collection.
