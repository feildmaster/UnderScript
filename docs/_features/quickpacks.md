---
title: Quick open packs
date: 2018-10-26
version: 0.17.0
---

Quickly open booster packs with Shift (all) and Ctrl (one).

Opening several packs at once shows a progress bar, with a button to stop.

Plugins can open packs too with [underscript.openPacks()]({% link _api/underscript/packs.open.md %}).
