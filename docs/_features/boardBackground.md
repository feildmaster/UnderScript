The game gives your side of the board a background based on your rank. Pick which ranks' backgrounds you would rather see with "Priority" in the Board Background section of the Game settings.

It is a list of the ranks, Legend, Ultimate Master, High Master, Master, Diamond, Emerald, Gold, Iron and Copper, plus None, that you put in your own order. When a game starts, UnderScript uses the first rank in your list that you have reached. If that is None, the board background is turned off.

"Notify" shows a toast telling you which background was set, or that it was turned off.
