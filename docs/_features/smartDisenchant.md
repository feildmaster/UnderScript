A Smart Disenchant button next to your dust on the Crafting page disenchants cards in bulk. It opens a window with four choices, each showing how much dust you would get:

* All Normal disenchants every normal card you own
* All Shiny disenchants every shiny card you own
* Prioritize Normal counts normal and shiny cards together and disenchants the extra shiny cards, along with any normal cards above the maximum
* Prioritize Shiny does the same the other way around, disenchanting the extra normal cards

DETERMINATION cards and shiny BASE cards are never disenchanted.

It is turned off by default. To use it, turn off "Disable Smart Disenchanting" in the Crafting section of the Library settings.
