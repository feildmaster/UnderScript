title: Game found notification
date: 2019-06-01
version: 0.27.0
notice: This used to be a vanilla feature until removed by Onutrem
---

When a match is found while you are in another tab or window, your browser shows a notification.

The first time you open the Play page, a toast asks if you would like to be notified. Choose "Request Permission" and allow notifications in your browser, or dismiss the toast to not be asked again.
