Current Hotkeys:
* Space - end turn
* Middle Click - end turn
* Escape - open or close the UnderScript menu

Hotkeys don't fire while you are typing in a text box. Ending the turn only works on your turn, and each way of ending it can be turned off in the Game settings. Plugins can add their own hotkeys with [plugin.hotkey]({% link _api/plugin/hotkey.md %}).

Holding CTRL or SHIFT while clicking does extra things in several places, those are listed on their own pages, like [Buy multiple packs]({% link _features/buyPacks.md %}) and [Craft to max]({% link _features/craftMax.md %}).
